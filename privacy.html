Pathfinder Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Pathfinder is a free exploration app. Your walking history, photos, videos and progress stay on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and the developer runs no server that receives your data.

What is stored on your device

Explored map cells, distance, XP, badges, quests, saved places, recorded trips, scan history, photos and videos (including the optional vault), settings, and cached map tiles. You can delete these inside the app (Settings, and each screen), or by uninstalling the app.

Permissions

  • Location: to lift the fog, show your position and record trips. Trip recording can continue in the background while a notification is visible.
  • Camera and microphone: for photos, videos and the code scanner. Audio is used only in video mode.
  • Notifications: to show the trip recording notification.

Third-party services

Some features contact free public services. Those services receive the information needed to answer the request and handle it under their own policies:

  • Map tiles: the area you view (OpenStreetMap, OpenTopoMap, OpenFreeMap, Esri satellite imagery).
  • Place search: the text you type (Nominatim, Photon).
  • Nearby places and landmarks: the coordinates of your pin or position (Overpass API).
  • Weather and elevation: coordinates (Open-Meteo).
  • Product lookup: the barcode you scan (Open Food Facts).
  • 3D view: loads the map library from a public CDN (jsDelivr).

Children

Pathfinder is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.
